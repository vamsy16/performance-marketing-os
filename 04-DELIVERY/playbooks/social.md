# Social Media Service Delivery Playbook

How to deliver social media management for solar companies.

---

## What Clients Actually Want (vs What You Think)

They don't want "posts." They want:
- **Leads** (form fills, calls, WhatsApp messages)
- **Trust** (looks like a professional company)
- **Consistency** (they don't have to think about it)

Design every post to either **educate** or **prove**.

---

## Monthly Package: 20 Posts (5/week)

### Content Mix (The 4-3-2-1 Rule)
- **4 Educational** — how solar works, savings math, tips
- **3 Proof** — installations, testimonials, before/after
- **2 Engaging** — polls, questions, myth-busting
- **1 Promotional** — offer, subsidy deadline, CTA

### Weekly Production Workflow (60-90 min, batch it)

**Monday (40 min):**
1. Open Arena → paste carousel prompt from library
2. Generate content for 5 posts (Mon-Fri)
3. Copy into Google Sheet → review/edit

**Tuesday (30 min):**
1. Design posts in Canva (free) — use a template, just swap text
2. Export all 5 at once

**Wednesday (15 min):**
1. Schedule in Buffer (free, 3 channels) or Meta Business Suite
2. Write captions with hashtags
3. Done for the week

---

## Canva Template Setup (Do Once)
Create these templates in Canva (free):
1. **Educational carousel** — clean, brand colors
2. **Testimonial/proof post** — photo + quote overlay
3. **Tip of the week** — big number/text
4. **Offer/promo** — bold, urgency-driven
5. **Reel cover** — bold text thumbnail

Reuse these forever. Just change text + photos.

## What to Post (Copy-Paste Ideas)

### Educational
- "Your ₹5,000/month electricity bill → ₹500 with solar. Here's the math ↓"
- "3 things to check before installing rooftop solar in [CITY]"
- "Does solar work during monsoon? We checked. ↓"
- "PM Surya Ghar subsidy: up to ₹78,000 off. Here's how to claim it."
- "Solar panel lifespan: 25 years. Payback: 4-6 years. Do the math."

### Proof
- Before/after installation photos
- Customer video testimonial (even 15 sec on phone)
- "This family in [AREA] now generates more electricity than they use ↓"
- Installation timelapse (even phone footage works)

### Engaging
- "Myth: Solar doesn't work in winter. Fact? ↓"
- Poll: "What's stopping you from going solar? A) Cost B) Trust C) Didn't consider it"
- "Guess how much this 5kW system saves per month" (then reveal)

### Promotional
- "Free site survey this month — only 5 slots left"
- "Subsidy deadline approaching. Book your install before [DATE]."
- "We just opened bookings for [CITY] installations — DM 'SOLAR' to start"

## Reporting (Monthly)

| Metric | Where to Find It |
|---|---|
| Follower growth | Instagram/Facebook Insights |
| Reach / Impressions | Platform Insights |
| Engagement rate | (Likes + comments + saves) / reach |
| Profile visits | Insights |
| Link clicks | Insights or Bitly |
| DMs / inquiries received | Manually count |

Report format:
1. "We grew from X to Y followers (+Z%)"
2. "Top post this month: [topic] — [reach] reached"
3. "Generated X direct inquiries"
4. "Next month we'll focus on [strategy]"

---

## Pricing Psychology

When clients ask "why so many posts?" — reframe:

> "You don't pay for posts. You pay for a system that turns strangers into homeowners who call you. The posts are just the fuel."

Charge for the OUTCOME (leads, trust, consistency), not the SEATS (number of posts).

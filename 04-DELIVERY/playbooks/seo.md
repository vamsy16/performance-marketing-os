# SEO Service Delivery Playbook

How to deliver SEO for solar companies — step by step.

---

## Month 1: Audit + Foundation

### Step 1: Full SEO Audit (Day 1-3)
Use these FREE tools:
- **Google PageSpeed Insights** — speed check
- **Google Search Console** — if they have it (ask for access)
- **Google Business Profile** — check completeness
- **Ubersuggest free** (3 searches/day) or **Arena** — keyword research
- **Screaming Frog free** (500 URLs) — site crawl

Audit checklist:
- [ ] Website loads under 3 seconds on mobile?
- [ ] Title tags include "[City] solar" keywords?
- [ ] Meta descriptions written?
- [ ] Google Business Profile: photos, hours, services, posts?
- [ ] NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistent across directories?
- [ ] Blog exists and updated within last 60 days?
- [ ] Mobile responsive?
- [ ] SSL certificate (https)?
- [ ] Local schema markup?

### Step 2: Keyword Research (Day 3-5)
Use Arena with this prompt (from prompt library):
> "Research the top 30 search terms homeowners in [CITY] use for solar panels..."

Organize keywords:

| Type | Example | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Transactional | "solar panel installer near me" | Ready to buy |
| Commercial | "best solar company in [CITY]" | Comparing |
| Informational | "solar panel subsidy 2026 India" | Researching |
| Local | "rooftop solar [neighborhood]" | Specific area |

### Step 3: Google Business Profile Optimization (Day 5-7)
- Primary category: "Solar Energy Company"
- Add all services with descriptions
- Upload 20+ photos (installations, team, equipment)
- Enable messaging
- Create first 4 Google Posts
- Set up Q&A (pre-answer 10 common questions)
- Ask 5 past customers for reviews (give them a direct link)

## Month 2-3: Content Engine

### Weekly rhythm
| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Mon | Generate blog post via Arena, edit, publish |
| Wed | Optimize GBP (add photo, post update) |
| Fri | Check rankings, build 1 citation |

### Blog topics that rank for solar
1. "Solar Panel Price in [CITY] 2026 — Complete Guide"
2. "PM Surya Ghar Subsidy: How to Apply in [STATE]"
3. "How Much Electricity Can Solar Save in [CITY]?"
4. "Best Solar Panels for [CITY]'s Climate"
5. "Rooftop Solar vs. Open Ground — What's Better in [CITY]?"
6. "Solar During Monsoon — Performance Facts"
7. "Commercial Solar for [CITY] Businesses — ROI Breakdown"
8. "[CITY] Electricity Rates 2026 — Why Solar Makes Sense Now"

### Blog production workflow (30 min per post)
1. Copy blog prompt from Arena prompt library
2. Paste into Arena with keyword + city filled in
3. Review output — fix anything factually wrong about local pricing/policy
4. Add internal links to service pages
5. Add 2-3 images (use free stock from Unsplash/Pexels)
6. Publish on client's website
7. Share on client's social media
8. Log in your tracker

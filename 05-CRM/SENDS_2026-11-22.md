## Three things worth knowing

**Eagle Solar Systems got its follow-up before its first touch.** The step-1
follow-up went at 10:25 in thread `1a1e1c3fc3a0baea`; a *separate* first-touch
email went at 13:13 in a new thread `1a1fe7b74d1dcb75`. Gmail is unambiguous
about the timestamps. Recorded as it happened — the second send is stored under
`extra_sends` rather than being forced into a step slot or reordered to look
tidy.

**Sree Bhalaji Industries belongs to no sequence.** It has a real two-step
thread (initial 16 Nov, follow-up 22 Nov) but no entry in
`sequence_manifest.json`, so logging it against a sequence would have meant
inventing one. It appears in `send_log.csv` only.

**Four sequences only ever had a step 0 built.** Growcell, Roof Rays, Akhil and
Manasaa were created with a single step record, so advancing `next_step` past
that point has nothing to send. They are now `live-due` with a note saying the
follow-up steps must be generated first. They are *not* marked finished — a
one-step sequence is not a completed sequence.

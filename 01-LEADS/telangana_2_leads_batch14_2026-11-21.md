**Corrections made by the 3rd-eye pass** (this is why the pass exists):

1. The Telugu ad copies were first recorded with an English gloss appended inside
   the ad-text field, which the PDF printed under the heading *"the complete text
   of every live ad exactly as the Meta Ad Library shows it."* That was a **false
   statement of verbatim fidelity**. The gloss was removed; the field is now
   strictly verbatim and the translation sits in a separate labelled note.
2. The PDF said "2 distinct live ads" while six were running. The label now reads
   *"2 ads reproduced (6 live on the page)"*.
3. The claude-ads **M30 "no social proof"** control was firing on the Telugu ads
   because the keyword scan is English-only. The ads do claim 10,000+ customers.
   `ads_audit_controls.py` now lets a report carry an `ad_copy_note` explaining
   that, and this report does.
4. "The counters render zeros" was over-broad — true on the **home page**, false
   on **/about**, which shows 1000+/1200+/850+/1100+. Rewritten as the
   three-way contradiction it actually is.
5. The `/about` lorem-ipsum and the uncaptioned `/Projects` gallery were missed
   on first read and added.

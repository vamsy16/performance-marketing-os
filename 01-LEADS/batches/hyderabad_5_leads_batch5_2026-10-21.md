## Why Telugu queries are the discovery tool now

English `solar hyderabad` is exhausted — every advertiser on that page is already
staged, drafted or sent (Venus, Sri Balaji, ORBIT, AB Solar, Suryon, Green Carbon
Hub, Evergrid, Solox, Truzon, Punarvi, Varahi, Ksquare, Twilight…). But the
**Telugu solar-subsidy feed is full of small installers buying Hyderabad leads
that no one else is pitching to** — and most of them leak the click straight into
Facebook lead forms or WhatsApp, which is exactly the gap this repo sells.

### Per-lead notes (all read live 2026-10-21)

**1. Valcan Solar** — Secunderabad-based (Vikrampuri Colony), since 2012, ISO
9001:2008, and currently running its biggest campaign: the MEGA UTSAV bumper-draw
with a hard deadline (offer valid to 24 Nov, draw 30 Nov). Two ads, two
destinations: one clicks through to valcansolar.com, the other into a Facebook
lead form — half the spend lands on a page they own, half lands inside Facebook
with no pixel and no retargeting. The live site has three quotable breaks a paid
visitor hits immediately: the "Our Achievements" counters all read **0** (0K+
projects, 0+ years, 0% success rate), the contact block's email reads
**marketing@vulcansolar.in** (misspelled — the header shows info@valcansolar.com),
and the contact block's phone link (+91 9010008373) differs from the displayed
number (+91 90300 33577). On a draw with a deadline, a broken enquiry path is a
lost entry. Angle: unify both ads on one deadline-driven landing page, fix the
counters and the contact details before the 30 Nov draw.

**2. Eastman New Energy** — the big fish of the batch, flagged like Greentek.
This is the solar arm of the Eastman group: **800 MW in-house panel
manufacturing**, part of EAPL (25 GW group-wide), 1000+ distributors, 400+ OEM
partners. The 4 live Telugu ads sell a **B2C** promise (₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar
subsidy + extra state subsidy, "reduce your bill to zero") but every click lands
on a **manufacturer's product catalogue** — on-grid/off-grid/hybrid inverter
pages, 50W–590W panel ranges — with the enquiry form behind reCAPTCHA and a
character-limited subsidy field. Every ad ends on the short-code 80016 80016, a
call-centre funnel. Angle: a dedicated residential PM Surya Ghar landing page
subsidy calculator + quote form, lower form friction, pixel + retargeting on the
offer. Treat the scale difference explicitly in the pitch.

**3. Ellvin Energy** — the newest advertiser in the batch and the purest leak:
**6 ads, one Telugu creative**, every click into a Facebook lead form. The
company was **incorporated only in August 2025** (CIN U35105AP2025PTC120835,
directors Vijaya Bhaskar and Nirmala Gangavarapu) — a one-year-old installer
buying Telugu leads into Telangana from a Tadepalli (Guntur, AP) HQ. The
creative's "3KW plant at zero cost for the first 1 crore people" promise has no
landing page behind it to honour it, no second angle, and no retargeting pool —
every rupee of spend is a lead form inside Facebook and nothing else. Angle: an
owned Telugu eligibility-check landing page, pixel + CAPI so the spend builds a
retargetable audience, and a second creative angle to test.

**4. Kapil Power & Infra** — a heavyweight: the Kapil Group is a Hyderabad
household name since 1981, 25+ years in solar, incorporated 2004, TGREDCO-
empanelled (**TSRE180167**), offices in Kapil Towers (Financial District). Both
live Telugu ads send the click into a Facebook lead form — so none of the paid
traffic ever sees the full EPC site (on-grid, off-grid, rooftop, ground-mount,
street lighting, solar fencing, O&M, DISCOM/MNRE paperwork handled). The "1000+
happy customers" claim in the ad has no matching proof page for a click that
never reaches the site. Angle: a Hyderabad residential landing page that carries
the group's track record (1981, 25+ yrs, TSRE180167, 1000+ customers), plus
pixel + retargeting so the Meta spend builds an owned audience.

**5. Solar6** — Kukatpally installer (Allwyn Colony, opp SBI Bank), over a decade
of experience, running both solar6.co.in and solar6.in, and advertising a Dasara
offer (zero down payment, ₹78,000 + subsidy, free Amaron inverter & battery,
authorised partners Adani/Microtek/Waaree/SBI/Union/Canara). The live ad sends
every click to **WhatsApp** — no site visit, no email capture, nothing to
measure or retarget. The ad's phone (9052 144 777) is different from the site's
(+91 9912355344) — two numbers, two funnels. The live site's "Success by the
Numbers" counters all read **0** (0+ projects, 0+ customers, 0+ team, 0+ years)
next to an "India's #2 Leading Rooftop Solar Company" claim, and it still carries
template demo links (wordpress.vecurosoft.com/energiso) plus a project image
credited to **Maxwatt Solar** — another company's photo. Angle: an owned offer
landing page with name/phone/locality captured *before* the WhatsApp hand-off,
fix the counters and the photo credit, align the two numbers.

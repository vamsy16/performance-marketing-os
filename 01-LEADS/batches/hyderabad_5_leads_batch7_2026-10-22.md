### Per-lead notes (all read live 2026-10-22)

**1. MrSolar (mrsolar.in)** — the single-advertiser case: one Hyderabad
customer-review video running since 5 May 2026, over five months on the same
creative, with the click going through an fb.me wrapper. Meanwhile the site
(fetched today) already has everything a subsidy-motivated click wants — the
PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 subsidy content with 300 free units, a savings
calculator, an eligibility form, an ISO 9001:2015 badge, and city pages for
Hyderabad plus seven other Telangana towns. The contact paths disagree with
each other: the contact page lists info@mrsolar.in / contact@mrsolar.in with
+91 8500225885, while the site's own WhatsApp button uses +91 85009 11912.
Angle: five months of spend, one creative, zero owned landing pages.

**2. Arcedo Systems (arcedo.net)** — one company running as two brands. The
"Arcedo Systemss" page runs three live ads with three different destinations
(fb.me, solar.suryon.in, a Messenger document) — none of them a page on
arcedo.net. The Sep 30 residential ad carries **Suryon Solar Systems**
branding on the Arcedo page, and the separate "Suryon" Facebook page runs the
same brand's ads with Arcedo's own contact details (info@arcedo.net, 844 844
8552) in the copy — confirmed today from the live ads. The highest-ticket
offer in the account (solar + battery for factories at zero capex, "cut power
bills by up to 50%") points at a Messenger document. arcedo.net itself is the
large-scale EPC site (rooftop, ground-mount, floating solar, O&M, MEP) while
the ads sell ₹78,000-subsidy homes and commercial OPEX. Public records: Arcedo
Systems Private Limited, CIN U35105TS2025PTC193163 (incorporated 21 Jan 2025,
ROC Hyderabad), TGREDCO-empanelled and TGNPDCL-listed — none of which appears
in the ad copy. Angle: pick one brand and one funnel.

**3. Arkahub (arkahub.in)** — the best-structured paid path in the batch: two
live Telugu ads (Oct 14–15) and **both point at owned pages**, the second at a
dedicated Hyderabad landing page (arkahub.in/hyderabad). The two ads also test
two genuinely different angles — aesthetics ("solar that doesn't spoil the look
of your home", hidden wires, flush-mount custom GI structure) versus paperwork
("price, 30-year warranty and 5 years free maintenance in writing"). The gap is
language: both ads are Telugu while the site, city selector and consultation
form are English-first. The consumer brand of Interglacial Renewable
Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (the name on the MNRE portal), Kae Capital / Antler /
Sparrow-backed after a $2M seed round, 900+ customers claimed — trust assets
that never appear in the ad copy. Registered office is Bengaluru; Hyderabad is
one of four operational cities. Angle: mirror the ad language on the Hyderabad
page.

**4. Orbit Solar (orbitsolar.in)** — the longest continuously-running
advertiser of the batch: five live ads, the oldest started 6 Nov 2025 —
residential ("They trusted us. You can too", Dec 10, six creatives), two
commercial/industrial ROI ads (Nov 6), an industrial Telangana ad (May 14) and
a new On-Grid Solar + UPS combo (Sep 4). Every click goes to fb.me or WhatsApp;
not one ad points at a page on orbitsolar.in, even though the site (fetched
today) claims MNRE-approved vendor status and carries brochures, a calculator
and an enquiry form. The install-count claims contradict each other across live
ads: "1000+ installations across Hyderabad" (residential) vs "500+ Successful
Installations Across Telangana" (commercial) vs "1,000+ industrial clients
across India" (industrial). No email is published anywhere on the site.
**Note:** Orbit Solar previously appeared in this repo only as a *competitor
sample* inside earlier reports (first verified 2026-10-18) — this is its first
row as a lead. Angle: a year of spend with no owned funnel and three different
company sizes.

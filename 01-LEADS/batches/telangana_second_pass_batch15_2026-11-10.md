## The one new advertiser, and why it fails

**`sarvaadhigreenenergies`** — Library ID `1726095265901439`, started
**8 November 2026**, page `sarvaadhigreenenergies` on Instagram (page id
1102263706299903). Confirmed by opening the ad directly at `?id=`.

It was surfaced by the `solar water heater telangana` query.

**Why it does not qualify:**

- **The ad has no copy at all.** The card is a single image with no primary
  text, no headline and no description. There is nothing to audit and nothing
  to quote back to the advertiser.
- **No website is identifiable from the ad.** The only link is
  `api.whatsapp.com/send` — a bare WhatsApp URL with no phone number appended,
  so it does not even resolve to a conversation.
- **The business cannot be identified from the ad.** A web search for
  "Sarvaadhi Green Energies" returned no matching company; the results were
  other, unrelated solar businesses. Without a website there is no way to
  confirm who the advertiser is, where they operate, or whether they are in
  Telangana at all.

The standing gate is "live Meta ads **plus a resolvable website** plus a
reachable email". This advertiser meets the first condition only. No audit PDF
and no outreach is produced.

## Conclusion

Across two passes, 25 city queries, 3 Telugu queries, 16 state-level and
segment queries, 17 company-name probes against the TGREDCO register, and both
editions of that register, the Telangana market outside Hyderabad has produced
**5 audited leads and 1 disqualified one**. The pattern is consistent and it is
worth stating plainly: the paid-advertising solar market in Telangana outside
Hyderabad is held by a small number of Hyderabad-headquartered advertisers
(Lumisun, Sri Balaji, Truzon, Eastman, Suryon, Orbit) whose campaigns cover the
tier-2 cities as target locations rather than as markets. The tier-2 EPCs on
the government register are real businesses with real addresses, phones and
emails, but they are not running paid Meta ads.

**Recommendation for the next pass, if the owner wants one:** stop probing for
ads in tier-2 Telangana. The two vectors that have not been tried are
(a) Google Ads Transparency for the same tier-2 names, and (b) accepting
register-only leads and pitching them on the *absence* of paid presence rather
than auditing existing spend — which would need the owner's explicit approval,
because it changes the offer from "here is what your ads are doing" to "here is
what your competitors' ads are doing while you have none".

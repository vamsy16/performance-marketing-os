### Per-lead notes (all read live 2026-10-23)

**1. Krisol Solar (krisolsolar.com)** — the busiest fresh campaign in the
batch: three ads launched inside five days (16–21 Oct) behind two angles — the
stacked ₹78,000 subsidy + ₹22,000 "Krisol Discount", and the ₹3,000–20,000
monthly bill-saving story — and every click lands on an fb.me link inside
Facebook. The site already has what the ads need (contact form, a "Government
Subsidy Available" popup form, a PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 FAQ, product pages for
rooftops/water heaters/street lights/pumps). Contact hygiene issue: the contact
page publishes krisolsolarinfra@gmail.com while the footer publishes
sales@krisolsolar.com. Krisol Solar Infra Pvt Ltd is a subsidiary of Satya
Enterprises, a Kompally solar dealer; the site claims service across Telangana,
AP, Karnataka & Maharashtra.

**2. PMP Solar Power (pmpsolar.com)** — one ad, but the most concrete offer in
the whole lead set: fixed festive pricing, subsidy-inclusive, "no negotiation",
Hyderabad homes only. The click goes to an fb.me link, so that pricing has
nowhere to land. The site's own proof is strong: 36 projects delivered, 420 kW
installed, 24 localities, largest plant 230 kW (D-Mart, Manneguda), named
residential installs across Kompally, Bachupally, Alwal, Rampally, Bandlaguda,
Tirumalgiri and Nizamabad. PMP is three businesses from one Kapra office (Solar,
Fabrication, Electrical) — the single creative speaks only to homeowners, so the
commercial/factory buyer has no ad at all. Enquiries run through a personal
Gmail (sreepooji27@gmail.com).

**3. Dakshin Solar Power Systems (dakshinsolar.com)** — a single generic
awareness ad (live since 16 Oct) whose click opens a WhatsApp chat. The site is
the most specification-heavy in the batch: TOPCon panels, Polycab/Growatt
inverters, an 8-component turnkey list with 30-year panel / 10-year inverter /
5-year free service warranties, a PM Surya Ghar ₹78,000 section with a "Scheme
Ends 31st March 2027" countdown, and a completed-projects grid — none of which
the ad reflects or the click can reach. One page-quality flag worth their
attention: the homepage stat block ships as "0 Yrs / 0%" in the page source and
depends on script animation to fill in.

**4. 3S Solutions (3ssol.in)** — six active ads, four hooks, and the sharpest
funnel problem of the batch: the newest bill-saving creative (₹4,280 → ₹187
Hyderabad customer story) pushes a savings calculator hosted at
**3scalculator.vercel.app** — a third-party domain, not 3ssol.in. The site
itself is content-rich (on-grid/off-grid guides, Hyderabad locality pages,
contact form) and the trust assets are real — Growatt distributor in India,
Vikram Solar distributor for AP & TG, TGREDCO-empanelled (reg. TSRE240328) —
but none of it appears in any ad, and none of the ads sends anyone to the site.
Contact numbers are split three ways across ad and site.

**5. Hydromo (hydromo.in)** — the structural mismatch case: Hydromo's core
trade is water and wastewater treatment (its own page title says so), yet it is
buying commercial-solar ads across AP & Telangana. Both solar ads route to
fb.me links while the site's solar pages (solar-commercial,
renewable-energy-solutions, solar-panel-installation-in-hyderabad) sit unseen.
The unused trust assets are the biggest in the batch: Toyota, ITC, Larsen &
Toubro, TATA, GMR, Dr Reddy's, HPCL, Cyient, Avantel, GVK, Auro Realty, DPS and
Ankura Hospital logos, IGBC and ISO certification, and two 2025 sustainability
awards. Residential rooftop — where the ₹78,000 subsidy drives most Hyderabad
demand — has no creative at all.

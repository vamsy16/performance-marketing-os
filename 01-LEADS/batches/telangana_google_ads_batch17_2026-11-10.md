## New discovery vector — Google Ads Transparency Centre

`https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=IN&domain=<domain>` returns the advertiser
legal name, a Verified badge and the ad count for any domain. **Confirmed working** on
2026-11-10 (probe on `orbitsolar.in` → 8 ads, advertiser "Orbit Solar Energy System",
Verified).

This is a genuinely untried channel. It finds **Google-only advertisers the Meta Ad
Library cannot reach** — the decisive example this batch: `zenithenergy.com` was probed
in batch 9 on 2026-10-23 and recorded as a *zero-ad website probe (no live ads → not a
lead)*. It was dropped for having no live Meta ads. It has, in fact, been running **9
live Google ads** the whole time. Several of the tier-2 Telangana companies the Ad
Library returned nothing for are in the same position.

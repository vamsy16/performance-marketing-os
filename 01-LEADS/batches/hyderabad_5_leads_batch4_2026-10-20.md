## Why this batch looks different from batch 3

Batch 3 was "companies buying Meta ads and leaking the click". Today's re-scan of
the same query shows that pool is exhausted — Venus, Sri Balaji, ORBIT SOLAR,
AB Solar, Suryon, Green Carbon Hub, mrsolar.in, ARKA SOLAR POWER, Hysun, Krisol,
3S, Evergrid, Solox, Truzon, Punarvi and Ksquare are all already staged, drafted
or sent. So batch 4 goes the other way and targets the **established Hyderabad
EPCs with no paid social at all**: the pitch flips from "fix your ad funnel" to
"your competitors are buying the same Hyderabad homeowner on Meta while you rely
entirely on search and word of mouth". One active advertiser (Twilight) is
included as the bridge case.

### Per-lead notes (all read live today)

**1. Twilight Solar Pvt Ltd** — the only active advertiser in the batch, and a
textbook leak. Two creatives have run since **30 July 2026** (~12 weeks) and both
send the click into a Facebook lead form, so every rupee of spend lands on
Facebook's page, not twilightsolar.com. They own no retargeting pool, no landing
page to A/B test and no pixel data. Two concrete hooks from their own live site:
the homepage counters read **128+ installations / 119+ satisfied customers / 12+ MW**
while the ad copy claims "**280+ Happy Customers**" — the ad is promising more than
double what the site can prove; and the site's only published email is a personal
Gmail (`Prabhakar.katkam@gmail.com`), not a company address. Chengicherla
(500039) is far-east Hyderabad — a local-service radius worth targeting.
Caveat: only ~2 ad results, so this is a small advertiser; do not quote spend or
impression numbers (the library shows none).

**2. Four Solar Energy Systems Pvt. Ltd.** — the biggest name in the batch with
zero paid acquisition. Est. 2013, TGREDCO-empanelled (TSRE180027), the site now
claims **13+ years, 50 MW+ delivered, 1500+ satisfied clients**, 15+ gated
communities powered, and a 1521-government-schools Telangana order plus a 160 kW
Corporate Bhavan (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) job. Their residential page is a
well-built problem/solution matrix (approvals, roof types, monitoring app,
lifetime O&M) — good raw material that no paid traffic ever sees. Site is a
Bricks build with a working multi-city quote form (Hyderabad / Secunderabad /
Ranga Reddy / Medchal-Malkajgiri) and a chat widget. Angle: they already have the
proof and the content; they just have no paid channel pointed at it.

**3. Origian Power** — a Hyderabad/Anantapur EPC running on content marketing
alone. The site is a WordPress location-page farm (Hyderabad, Warangal, Nizamabad,
Karimnagar, Suryapet, Nellore, Vijayawada, Visakhapatam, Guntur, Tirupati,
Kurnool, Kadapa, Nandyal…) covering on-grid, off-grid, hybrid, water heating,
pumps, lighting and PM Suryaghar subsidy assistance. Contact page is live with
three phone lines and a Gmail address (`origian.ei@gmail.com` — no company
domain, despite owning origianpower.com **and** origianpower.in). Two offices in
Hyderabad (Nizampet 500090, Hitech City 500081). Angle: the pages exist but
nothing paid drives into them; a dedicated Hyderabad residential landing page +
Meta/Telugu creative would monetise the content they already rank with.

**4. Mamindla Energy Solutions LLP** — a young, fast-moving Warangal-based EPC
with a real Hyderabad branch (Madhapur 500081) and a claims-led site: "Govt.
Empanelled Vendor", 100+ installations, 10+ MW, 500+ happy customers, an
interactive "AI Solar Savings Calculator", a 5-step process graphic and named
project case studies (8 kW Kondapur residential, 25 kW Hanamkonda school,
50 kW LB Nagar industrial shed). Tier-1 panel claims (Waaree/TATA/Adani) and full
PM Surya Ghar paperwork handling. No Meta presence at all. Angle: they are
already doing the trust-building work on-page — paid acquisition is the missing
half, and Madhapur/Hitech City targeting would sit right next to their branch.

**5. Junna Solar Systems Pvt Ltd** — the oldest installer in the batch (incorporated
Dec 2012, now a public limited company, CIN U40106TG2012PLC084777),
TGREDCO-empanelled (TSRE180043) and a registered PM Surya Ghar vendor for
Telangana North, HQ in Kukatpally 500072, 13+ years, water-pump and rooftop lines,
and an active hiring pipeline (project managers, sales engineers). The live site is
a JavaScript app: the homepage renders almost nothing without JS, and in the served
HTML the floating WhatsApp button points at **`wa.me/undefined`** — a broken deep
link on the one channel a solar buyer is most likely to tap. Angle: a paid-social
funnel plus a working WhatsApp/lead path; the site work is the obvious first fix.

## Notes for whoever picks this up

- **Four of the five run no Meta ads**, so `ads_only_report.py` is the wrong tool
  for them (its gate flags "advertising but has no ads"). These need the **full
  audit** — website score, pixels, PageSpeed — via `run_pipeline.py` locally or
  the Actions tab, one company at a time:
  - `python run_pipeline.py https://www.foursolar.com/ "Four Solar" --city Hyderabad`
  - `python run_pipeline.py https://origianpower.com/ "Origian Power" --city Hyderabad`
  - `python run_pipeline.py https://www.m-e-solutions.co.in/ "Mamindla Energy Solutions" --city Hyderabad`
  - `python run_pipeline.py https://www.junnasolar.com/ "Junna Solar Systems" --city Hyderabad`
- **Twilight Solar** is the ads-only case (2 live Library IDs above) and can go
  through `ads_only_report.py` + `ads_only_outreach.py` exactly like batch 3.
- Twilight's ad-vs-site customer-count gap (280+ in the ad vs 128+/119+ on the
  site) is quotable because both numbers were read live today; the ~2-ad count is
  the whole library result, so make no spend/impression claims.
- All five rows are appended to `01-LEADS/leads.csv` with `status: new`.
- Greentek India Limited was verified as a lead (info@greentekindia.co.in,
  +91 89777 52285, Secunderabad, 28+ years, 3.2+ GW, TGREDCO TSRE180039, no Meta
  ads) but held out of the five: it is a public limited manufacturer, a much
  larger fish than the rest of the batch. Pitch it separately if a big-ticket
  target is wanted.

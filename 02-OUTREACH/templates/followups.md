## Rules
1. **Never send more than 3 follow-ups** — respect their time
2. **Every follow-up must add NEW value** — don't just "bump"
3. **The breakup email gets the highest reply rate** — people respond when you stop asking
4. **If they reply "not interested"** — say "Understood. Can I check back in 6 months?" and add to CRM with 6-month follow-up

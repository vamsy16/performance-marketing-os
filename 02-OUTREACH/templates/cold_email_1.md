# Cold Email Template 1 — Free Audit Hook (BEST OPENER)

**When to use:** First contact with a solar company you found online.
**Goal:** Get them to reply for a free audit. Don't sell anything yet.

---

Subject: Quick question about [COMPANY NAME]'s solar leads

Hi [OWNER NAME],

I was searching for solar installers in [CITY] and came across [COMPANY NAME].

I noticed a few things about your online presence that might be costing you leads — specifically [ONE SPECIFIC OBSERVATION, e.g., "you're not showing up in the Google Map Pack for 'solar installer near me'"].

I work with solar companies to fix exactly this and get them more qualified homeowner leads.

Would you be open to me recording a quick 5-minute video audit of your Google presence? No strings attached — it's yours to keep whether we work together or not.

Either way, keep up the great work with solar in [CITY].

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
Smart Pursuit
[YOUR PHONE]

---

## Personalization checklist (DO THIS before sending)
- [ ] Company name correct
- [ ] Owner name (find on LinkedIn/Google)
- [ ] City correct
- [ ] ONE specific observation (check their Google listing first)
- [ ] Your real phone number

## Why this works
- No pitch. Just value.
- "5-minute video audit" is low commitment
- "No strings attached" removes pressure
- Specific observation shows you actually looked

## Expected results
- 100 emails → 8-15 replies → 3-5 audits → 1-2 clients

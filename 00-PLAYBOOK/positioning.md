## Pricing psychology for solar owners

Solar owners think in **cost-per-lead** and **commission per installation**.
- Average rooftop solar deal: ₹3L–10L
- Their profit per deal: ₹50k–2L
- So paying you ₹30k for 5 leads that close 1-2 deals = they profit massively

**Always frame your price against their deal value, not your time.**

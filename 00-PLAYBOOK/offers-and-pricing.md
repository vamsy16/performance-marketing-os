## Pricing rules for a solo agency

1. **Never charge by the hour** — charge by outcome/retainer
2. **Start at 40% of market rate** for first 3 clients (portfolio building)
3. **Raise prices 20% every 3 clients**
4. **Minimum retainer: ₹10k/mo** — below that not worth the overhead
5. **Annual discount:** 2 months free if they pay 10 months upfront

## Payment terms

- Retainers: 50% advance, 50% on 1st of month
- Projects: 50% advance, 50% on delivery
- Use Razorpay free payment links (no setup fee) or UPI transfer

## Contracts

Use a simple 2-page agreement:
1. Scope of work (what you'll do)
2. Deliverables + timelines
3. Payment terms
4. 30-day notice period either way
5. You own the strategy, they own the accounts

*Don't skip contracts. Even simple ones prevent 90% of disputes.*

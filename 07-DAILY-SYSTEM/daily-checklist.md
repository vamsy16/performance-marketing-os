# Daily System — Built for Inconsistent Hours

You don't need 8 hours/day. You need a **priority system** that works in any time slot.

## The Golden Rule

> **Always do the money task FIRST.** Outreach > everything else.

---

## Pick Your Time Slot

### 🔥 If you have 30 minutes
Do ONE of these (rotate daily):

| Priority | Task | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send 10 cold emails | Pipeline = income |
| 2 | Follow up with 5 warm leads | Highest close rate |
| 3 | Post 1 social content piece | Visibility |
| 4 | Reply to any client messages | Retention |
| 5 | Update CRM statuses | Clarity |

### ⚡ If you have 1-2 hours
Add ONE of these:

| Task | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Run lead finder + review results | 30 min | New prospects |
| Record a video audit for a prospect | 20 min | Trust builder |
| Generate blog post via Arena + publish | 40 min | Client deliverable |
| Create 5 social posts via Arena + schedule | 40 min | Client deliverable |
| Write a proposal for a warm lead | 30 min | Revenue |

### 🚀 If you have 3+ hours (power session)
Do a **Batch Day**:

| Block | Time | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Batch 1 | 90 min | Send ALL outreach (20-30 emails/follow-ups) |
| Break | 15 min | — |
| Batch 2 | 90 min | Generate ALL content for the week via Arena |
| Break | 15 min | — |
| Batch 3 | 60 min | Client work (publish, optimize, report) |
| Wrap | 15 min | Update CRM, plan tomorrow |

---

## Weekly Non-Negotiables

Even in bad weeks, hit these minimums:

| Day | Minimum (even 15 min) |
|---|---|
| Mon | Send 10 cold emails |
| Tue | Follow up with 3 warm leads |
| Wed | Post 1 social content |
| Thu | Send 10 cold emails |
| Fri | Weekly review + plan next week |
| Sat | Content batch (if energy allows) |
| Rest | Truly rest. Burnout kills solo agencies. |

---

## Weekly Review (Every Friday, 30 min)

Answer these 5 questions in a notebook:

1. **How many new leads** did I add this week? (Target: 10+)
2. **How many outreach emails** did I send? (Target: 50+)
3. **How many replies** did I get? (Target: 5+)
4. **How many client deliverables** did I complete?
5. **What's my #1 priority** for next week?

---

## The "I Don't Feel Like It" Protocol

On days when motivation is zero:

1. Open CRM → run follow-ups command
2. Send just **3 emails** (takes 10 min)
3. Close laptop

3 emails keeps the habit alive. That's enough.

## Your Morning Startup Ritual (5 min)

Every workday, start with this exact sequence:

1. Open CRM → check follow-ups
2. Check email → respond to anything urgent
3. Pick ONE time slot from above
4. Set timer
5. Work until timer ends
6. Log what you did in CRM
7. Done. Feel good about showing up.
